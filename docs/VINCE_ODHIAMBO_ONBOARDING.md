## Onboarding schedule

### Day 1 — Context and access

- Review the ENOSX Technologies mission, products, public team page, and current company priority.
- Confirm access to the approved communication channel and the ENOSX AI GitHub repository.
- Review the repository README, `TEAM.md`, contribution conventions, issue labels, and open priority work.
- Create a personal list of questions, unknowns, and access gaps; escalate them to the founder rather than guessing.

**End-of-day output:** a one-page understanding of the role, current priorities, communication channels, and open questions.

### Day 2 — Founder operating rhythm

- Review the founder’s recurring meetings and priority-setting rhythm.
- Set up the action register with task, owner, due date, status, source, and next follow-up.
- Draft a lightweight meeting agenda and decision-log format.
- Agree on what requires immediate escalation versus the next daily check-in.

**End-of-day output:** a working action register and a proposed weekly calendar rhythm.

### Day 3 — ENOSX AI repository workflow

- Confirm the GitHub invitation for [enigmacxenosx/enosxai](https://github.com/enigmacxenosx/enosxai).
- Learn branch, pull-request, review, issue, and release conventions from the repository documentation.
- Triage the existing issue/task list with the founder or engineering lead; do not close, merge, or reprioritize technical work without the owner’s approval.
- Create a short repository coordination checklist.

**End-of-day output:** a repository coordination checklist and a first-pass list of open follow-ups.

### Day 4 — Coordination practice

- Prepare and circulate a meeting agenda before an approved team meeting.
- Capture decisions, owners, and due dates during the meeting.
- Send a concise recap and update the action register.
- Follow up on at least two non-sensitive commitments using the agreed communication channel.

**End-of-day output:** one completed meeting record and an updated action register.

### Day 5 — First weekly review

- Present the action register, completed follow-ups, blockers, and proposed improvements.
- Confirm the next week’s three highest-priority coordination outcomes.
- Agree on a recurring weekly review with the founder.
- Review confidentiality, public communications, GitHub permissions, and escalation boundaries.

**End-of-week output:** a five-day onboarding recap and a prioritized plan for week two.

## Founder-to-assistant communication workflow

### Daily check-in

Use one short check-in at the agreed start of the working day:

1. **Founder priorities:** the top three outcomes for the day.
2. **Vince’s coordination plan:** meetings, follow-ups, and expected outputs.
3. **Blockers:** anything requiring a decision, access, or clarification.
4. **End-of-day recap:** completed items, carried-forward items, and risks.

### Action register

Maintain one shared register with these fields:

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Task | A clear action beginning with a verb |
| Owner | One accountable person |
| Priority | P0 urgent, P1 this week, P2 planned |
| Due date | Specific date or “awaiting decision” only while awaiting a decision |
| Status | Not started, in progress, blocked, ready for review, complete |
| Next follow-up | The next observable action |
| Source | Meeting, message, issue, or decision record |
| Notes | Short context; never store passwords or sensitive personal data |

### Weekly review

At the weekly review, Vince prepares:

- Completed commitments and measurable outcomes.
- Open tasks grouped by owner and priority.
- Decisions needed from the founder.
- Blockers and risks, including stale tasks.
- The next week’s three coordination outcomes.

The founder confirms priorities, resolves escalations, and approves changes to role ownership or public commitments.

### Escalation rules

Escalate immediately when a matter involves security, personal data, payments, legal commitments, public statements, access permissions, a customer incident, or a deadline that may be missed. For routine coordination, capture the item in the action register and raise it at the next check-in.
